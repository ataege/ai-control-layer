# Source register and licenses (RS-06)

Owner: researcher, document owner and presenter. Status: **started 3 October 2026.** Every external
statement in the presentation and the handoff cites an entry here, and each entry backs "only the
narrow factual statement it supports" (roadmap RS-06). S1 to S10 keep the report's numbering
("Sources and evidence register", report 1.2); S11 onwards are added by the researcher.

**content checked** (the statement we rely on was read on the page), or **summary only** (read through
a search summary; reread the page before a slide quotes it).
